How it works

Four steps. Zero card networks.

From plan creation to merchant payout — fully automated, fully on-chain.

01
Create a plan
Define billing amount, cycle, and trial period. A dedicated smart contract deploys automatically on Base.
02
User subscribes
Subscribers connect via Privy embedded wallets — no MetaMask needed. One-click authorization to the billing contract.
03
Billing runs
USDC is pulled automatically each billing date. Failed payments are retried with smart grace periods.
04
Merchant gets paid
Funds flow directly to your wallet. Track revenue, payouts, and subscriber health in real time.
