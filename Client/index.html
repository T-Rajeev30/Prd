How it works

From plan creation
to on-chain settlement

01
Create a plan
Define billing cycles, amounts, and trial periods from the merchant dashboard. A smart contract deploys automatically.
02
User subscribes
Subscribers connect via Privy embedded wallets — no MetaMask required. One-click authorization to the contract.
03
Billing runs
USDC is pulled automatically on each billing date. Failed payments are retried with configurable grace periods.
04
Merchant paid
Funds flow directly to your wallet. Track payouts, revenue, and subscriber health in real time.
