Our Story

Where Mariposa
Comes to Gather

What began with just 11 tables and a seasonal patio has grown into one of Mariposa's most beloved dining destinations. Nestled at the corner of 6th and Highway 140, Fredrick's of Savourys offers a warm, refined space with 22 tables year-round.

The Ansel Adams Gallery from Yosemite graciously hangs artwork from their featured photographers throughout the restaurant — because here, it's all about the trees, great food, and genuine hospitality.
